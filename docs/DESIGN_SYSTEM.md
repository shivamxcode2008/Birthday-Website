# Design System

## Overview
The design system establishes a premium, elegant foundation with playful, cute accents. It prioritizes mobile-first usability, readability, and a cohesive visual language that can gracefully accommodate personal content.

## Color Direction
The palette should be soft, inviting, and sophisticated, avoiding harsh contrasts or generic primary colors.
- **Backgrounds:** Soft pastel palette (e.g., cream, blush, soft pink, warm off-white).
- **Foreground/Text:** A subtle darker accent color (e.g., deep charcoal, warm espresso, or dark muted plum) for high readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Accents:** Gentle gradients, soft glows for interactive elements, and delicate pastel pops for decorative items (hearts, stars).

## Typography Hierarchy
Typography is crucial for the "premium stationery" feel. Keep the number of distinct fonts minimal (2-3 maximum).
- **Display / Serif (The Emotion):** Used for major emotional headings (e.g., "Happy Birthday", "A Letter For You"). Should feel elegant and sophisticated.
- **Sans-Serif (The Foundation):** Used for body copy, UI elements, and button text. Must be highly legible on small mobile screens.
- **Handwritten / Script (The Personal Touch):** Used sparingly for personal accents, signatures, or tiny notes/doodles in the scrapbook section.

## Spacing & Layout
- **Generous Whitespace:** Allow content to breathe. Do not cram elements together. Whitespace contributes heavily to the "premium" feel.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Consistent vertical margins to guide the eye down the mobile screen.
- **Safe Zones:** Keep interactive elements within comfortable thumb reach on mobile devices.

## UI Components

### Cards (Memories, Gifts, Letter)
- **Shape:** Rounded corners (not harsh right angles) to feel soft and approachable.
- **Depth:** Layered paper effects using very subtle, gentle drop shadows. Avoid heavy, dark shadows.
- **Textures:** Consider a very faint paper grain texture for the letter or envelope elements, applied subtly.

### Buttons & Interactions
- **Size:** Large, comfortable touch targets (minimum 44x44px). No tiny buttons.
- **Style:** Pill-shaped or softly rounded rectangles. Solid pastel fills or delicate outlines depending on primary vs. secondary action.
- **Feedback:** Micro-interactions on tap (e.g., slight scale down, soft glow, or gentle ripple) to feel responsive and alive.

## Decorative Elements
- **Usage:** Used intentionally as accents to make the experience feel alive, wholesome, and fun.
- **Types:** Cute doodles, stars, sparkles, balloons, cake, gifts, flowers, ribbons, and cute character reactions. (Hearts can be used sparingly as cute decorative elements).
- **Prohibited:** Absolutely no romantic symbolism (e.g., kissing/couple imagery, romantic red roses, couple illustrations).
- **Execution:** Can be animated (e.g., subtle floating particles, little pop animations), but must remain in the background and not distract from the primary content. The site must look beautiful even if these are removed.

## Mobile-First Principles
- **Viewport:** Designed for vertical, portrait orientation.
- **Gestures:** Utilize native-feeling gestures where appropriate (swipe to turn memory cards, tap to open).
- **No Overflow:** Prevent accidental horizontal scrolling.
- **Responsive Media:** Images and containers must adapt to varying mobile screen widths seamlessly.

## Premium vs. Playful Balance
- **Premium (70%):** Dictates the layout, typography, color palette, whitespace, and transition easing.
- **Playful (30%):** Injected through the gift-opening mechanic, candle interaction, micro-animations, and personalized copy.
